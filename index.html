Student voting.
Made simple.

Studying at CU Boulder? Find official information about registration, updating your address, and voting in Boulder. Whether you live on campus or off, start by checking your registration details.

Register or check status ↗ Find a vote center ↗

Election information from Boulder County Elections. This section is nonpartisan and does not endorse candidates or parties.

01 / Check your registration

Confirm your current address and registration details through Colorado's official voter portal.

02 / Register in person

Eligible Colorado residents can register and vote at a Vote Center through Election Day. Bring an accepted form of identification.

03 / Vote at the UMC

CU Boulder's University Memorial Center (UMC) is a Vote Center for the November 2026 election. Confirm its dates and hours with Boulder County before you go.

04 / Campus or off-campus address?

Use Boulder County's registration guidance to check which residential and mailing addresses to provide. Students attending college away from home should review their voting-residency options.
